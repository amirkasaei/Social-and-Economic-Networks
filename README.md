# Social & Economic Networks

<div align="center"><img src="asset/SocialNetworkAnalysis.png?raw=true" width="60%" /></div>

This course explores the structure and impact of social and economic networks. Students will learn key concepts such as network structure, information diffusion, strategic network formation, and economic effects. Using theoretical models, empirical analysis, and case studies, the course examines the role of networks in modern economies and societies

## Syllabus Titles

1. **Introduction to Networks (Complex, Brain, Social, …)**  
2. **Review of Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors, Covariance Matrix, & PCA**  
3. **Network Metrics**
   - Clustering Coefficient
   - Betweenness Centrality & Structural Holes
   - Triadic Closure, Cyclic Coefficient, Rich-club Coefficient, Homophily, etc.
4. **Motif, Isomorphism, and Graphlets**  
5. **Centrality and Graph Clustering (Laplacian Matrix)**  
6. **Community Detection Algorithms**  
7. **Random Graph Models**:
    - Erdös-Renyi
    - Small-world networks (Watts-Strogatz model, Newman-Watts model)
    - Kleinberg’s geographical model
8. **Scale-Free and Growth Models**:
    - Heavy-tailed distributions (Zipf & Pareto)
    - Barabasi-Albert model
    - Forest Fire model
    - Kronecker model
9. **Game on Networks**  
10. **Link Prediction**  
11. **Outbreak Detection**  
12. **Information and Influence Propagation**  
13. **Link Prediction and Recommendation**  
14. **Network Construction, Inference, and Deconvolution**  
15. **Games on Networks**  
16. **Graph Neural Networks**:
    - Message Passing & Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs)
17. **Knowledge Graphs and Meta Paths**
